## 2. Actions around the posts

Posting is less than half the work. What happens in the hour after a post, and every day between posts, decides who sees it and who starts a conversation.

### Every post day

| When | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Evening before | Proofread the post and the carousel PDF. Edits in the first hour may reset the test phase | 10 min |
| 15 min before | Comment on 3 to 5 posts from target accounts, from the founder's own profile, so the account is active when the post goes live | 10 min |
| Post goes live | Publish. For CP3 and M6, add the link as the first comment | 2 min |
| First 15 min | **Reaction block.** Stay online and reply to every comment in 2 to 3 sentences, ending with a question where it fits | 15 min |
| Up to 60 min | Check every 10 to 15 minutes and keep replying within 15 minutes. If a commenter fits the target profile, open their profile and add them to the lead tracker | 20 to 30 min |
| Same day | DM every target-profile commenter and everyone who sent "scope" or "early" (scripts in `plan.md`) | 10 min |

### Every working day, posting or not

| Action | Time |
|---|---|
| Comment on 5 to 8 posts from the target list (30 to 50 accounts). Add something of your own, such as an experience, a number or a counterpoint. Never pitch vAPI in someone else's comments | 15 to 20 min |
| Send connection requests with a short note to target-profile people who viewed a founder profile or commented (max 10 to 15 a day) | 5 min |
| Update the lead tracker: new leads, A/B/C grade, next step | 5 min |
